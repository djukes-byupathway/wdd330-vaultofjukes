About Us

Welcome to Vault of Jukes

Vault of Jukes grew out of a love for tabletop games, role-playing, miniature painting, and creating things together.

This site is a place for us to share the tools, projects, and ideas that come out of that hobby. Some are useful, some are experimental, and some are simply things we thought would be fun to build.

Whether we are rolling up a new character, painting a miniature, or working on our next project, the goal is simple: make tabletop gaming a little more fun.

Meet the Founders

The people behind the Vault.

Dr. Glen Jukes, DC..

Glen's introduction to the world of gaming came during an Boy Scout Encampment at Camp Pendleton many years ago. One of his fellow Scouts had brought a Dungeons and Dragons game, the old one with the blue dice. Needless to say, Glen was hooked. Now many years later, Glen still games with some of those fellows on the regular.

David Jukes

It may come as no surprise that as the younger brother, David was introduced to gaming through his older brother Glen. The brothers spent countless hours during their youth delving dungeons and living in worlds of fantasy. The shared experiences have formed a bond that will last eternity.

Amy Jukes

Amy is the power behind the scenes, and the real genius. She is a business wunderkund that is driving the fellows to get their act together. This endeavor would be impossible without her input.
