About Us

Welcome to Vault of Jukes

At the end of 2025, one of our founders, Glen Jukes, collapsed at work after suffering a stroke related to complications with his mechanical heart valve. What followed was high-risk heart surgery and a long road of physical and mental recovery.

As a way to reconnect, recover, and spend time doing something we all love, we decided to build something together around gaming.

That idea became Vault of Jukes.

For us, gaming has always been about more than dice and character sheets. It is imagination, creativity, laughter, friendship, and sometimes a much-needed escape when life gets hard.

Vault of Jukes is our way of sharing that love of gaming, fantasy, and friendship while celebrating life, creativity, and well-being. We hope you'll join us as we explore the endless possibilities waiting inside the Vault.

And we have to give a special shout-out to the incredible team at BSW Heart Hospital in Plano, Texas. You guys are the best!

Meet the Founders

The people behind the Vault.

Dr. Glen Jukes, DC..

Glen's introduction to the world of gaming came during an Boy Scout Encampment at Camp Pendleton many years ago. One of his fellow Scouts had brought a Dungeons and Dragons game, the old one with the blue dice. Needless to say, Glen was hooked. Now many years later, Glen still games with some of those fellows on the regular.

He is photo'ed with his wife Kathleen getting ready to leave stroke rehab with his #1 Patient trophy.

David Jukes

It may come as no surprise that as the younger brother, David was introduced to gaming through his older brother Glen. The brothers spent countless hours during their youth delving dungeons and living in worlds of fantasy. The shared experiences have formed a bond that will last eternity.

Dave is pictured here amidst his gaming paraphernalia, you may be able to make out some of his painting pile of shame stored in bins on the shelf behind him.

Amy Jukes

Amy is the power behind the scenes, and the real genius. She is a business wunderkund that is driving the fellows to get their act together. This endeavor would be impossible without her input.

Amy is not only the founder of multiple businesses and non-profits, she is also a dedicated mother. Here she is enduring a wrestling tournament with daughter number 4.
